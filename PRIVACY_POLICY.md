# Privacy Policy

**Discord Weather Bot**

**Last updated:** July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Discord Weather Bot handles information when you use it in Discord.

## 1. Information We Collect
Discord Weather Bot only processes the information it needs to answer your commands.

This may include:
- The command text you send to the bot.
- City names or other weather search terms you type.
- Basic Discord information needed for the bot to read your message and reply.
- Technical logs, if they are used for debugging or keeping the bot working.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use this information to:
- Show weather, climate, and forecast data.
- Respond to your commands.
- Fix bugs and keep the bot running.
- Improve the bot's basic performance and reliability.

## 3. What We Do Not Do
We do not sell your information.
We do not use the bot to collect personal profiles.
We do not try to track you outside of Discord.
We do not ask for passwords, payment details, or private account data.

## 4. Data Sharing
The bot sends city names and weather requests to third-party services such as OpenWeatherMap so it can return weather results.

We may also rely on Discord's own systems to deliver messages and run the bot.

We do not share user data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Data Retention
We do not promise that command data or logs will be kept forever.

If logs are kept, they are only used for maintenance, debugging, or security checks. They may be removed at any time.

## 6. Third-Party Services
Discord Weather Bot depends on Discord and OpenWeatherMap.

Their own privacy policies and terms also apply when you use the bot. We are not responsible for how those services handle data.

## 7. Children's Privacy
This bot is not made for children under the age allowed by local law. If you believe a child has shared personal information with the bot, please contact the bot owner.

## 8. Security
We use reasonable care to keep the bot working safely, but no online system is completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If you keep using the bot after a change, you agree to the updated policy.

## 10. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot owner or maintainer through the project's main repository or support channel, if one is provided.
